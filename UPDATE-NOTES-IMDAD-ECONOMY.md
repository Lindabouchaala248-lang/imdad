# IMDAD Premium 1.1 — Economy & Experience Update

This update preserves the approved Galaxy visual direction, Berry character, existing navigation and core page layout. It does not add new primary navigation tabs.

## Included in the prototype
- Marketplace product actions now explain the two paths available for every product: free promotion and acquiring a stock-linked demo asset. Customer-order creation remains available.
- My Assets now separates actual demo ownership records from saved products and displays a unique demo Asset ID, acquisition value, date and status.
- Internal Exchange is nested inside My Assets (not a new sidebar tab). Users can list a demo-owned asset for resale at an illustrative 15% discount and preview a demo ownership transfer.
- Wallet opens from the dashboard balance / quick action. It separates portfolio value, available balance and pending earnings; includes a withdrawal request form and request history.
- Sound effects are generated locally with Web Audio (soft interaction and success tones). Sound can be enabled/disabled in Profile settings; no audio files or external services are required.
- Minor responsive styling was added for the wallet, exchange and ownership cards.

## Important prototype limitations
All balances, asset purchases, listings, ownership transfers and withdrawal requests are local demo interactions stored in the browser. No legal ownership is created, no warehouse stock is reserved, no real secondary-market sale occurs, and no money is transferred. CCP/BaridiMob and bank transfer are shown as planned payout methods only. A production release needs a verified inventory ledger, legally reviewed ownership terms, authenticated backend transactions, fraud controls, settlement/reconciliation and an integrated licensed payout provider.
